# Disclaimer / 免责声明

## English

This repository documents independent security research into the privacy behavior of a
mobile application, conducted for educational and research purposes only.

- All analysis was performed on applications **legally installed on devices owned by the
  author**, and concerns only the author's **own accounts**.
- The scripts are **passive observers**: they read and record application behavior at
  runtime. They do not modify application data, do not attack any server, and do not
  transmit any data externally.
- This repository contains **no application binary, no decrypted IPA, and no copyrighted
  source code** — only the author's own analysis scripts and desensitized findings.
- All device identifiers (IDFV, vimei/vimsi/udid, UUIDs, tokens, IP) appearing in the
  findings are **desensitized / redacted**.
- Statements involving legal characterization use qualified language ("potentially",
  "may constitute"); final determination is left to the competent authorities. The author
  is not a legal professional.
- The study was conducted on version `7.28.60`. Behavior may differ in other versions, and
  the vendor may have changed it since.

If you are the vendor and believe anything here is inaccurate, open an issue — corrections
are welcome.
